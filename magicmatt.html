About Matt

From Paralyzing Anxiety to Canada's Most Followed Magician

Matt James couldn't always command a room. Not long ago, crippling stage anxiety made performing feel impossible. He knows what it feels like to sit in a crowd that isn't engaged, because he was once the person too nervous to engage them.

Frequently Asked Questions

I need approval from my boss or committee first.

That's exactly why we send a detailed proposal with pricing, services, highlight reel, and full client list. Everything your stakeholders need to say yes, in one document. Most planners get sign off the same day.

What if my group is skeptical of magic and mentalism?

That's what sets Matt apart. By blending storytelling, psychology, and immersive audience interaction, the show is built around authentic connection and memorable moments, not tricks. Even the most skeptical professionals walk away talking.

We're not sure if our budget is enough.

Every proposal is built around your event and budget. Tell Matt your range and he'll find the right format: stage show, strolling magic, keynote, or a combination. No pressure and no obligation.

Does Matt travel for events?

Yes. Matt performs at corporate events across North America. Travel and logistics are handled as part of the proposal. Just tell us your event location.

Is this appropriate for a diverse corporate audience?

Completely. Matt never uses political, ethnic, or suggestive humor. Every guest feels celebrated and respected regardless of background, age, or role.

How far in advance should I book?

The sooner the better. Peak dates like holiday parties, spring galas, and conference season fill months in advance. Submit the form and Matt's team confirms availability within 24 hours.
